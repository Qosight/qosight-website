Our Process

A structured path from first conversation to a community that keeps improving.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand the community, audience, goals and current problems.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Design the server architecture, member journey and required systems.

  3. 03

    Build

    Implement channels, categories, roles, permissions, automation and branding.

  4. 04

    Refine

    Test the community experience and fix issues before launch.

  5. 05

    Launch & Improve

    Launch the community and continue improving it based on its needs.
